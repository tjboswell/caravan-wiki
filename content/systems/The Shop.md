# The Shop

> Spend points on upgrades that grow your engine. Because points are also your [[systems/Gates|gate]] score, every purchase is an invest-vs-bank bet.

## How it works

The shop sells permanent upgrades bought with [[decisions/Points are score and shop currency|points]] (same pool as score — [[decisions/Points are score and shop currency]]). Starter offerings:

- **Market Multiplier** — +0.5× to all sell payouts (price scales per buy). The core snowball.
- **+1 Play / round** — more actions per round (capped).
- **Add a card** — thicken the [[systems/Action Deck|deck]] (a Bellows, Spice Run, Contagion, …).

**The tension:** spending sets you back on the current [[systems/Gates|gate]] but raises your earning rate. Since later gates are unreachable with a starter deck, reinvestment isn't optional — the skill is how much to invest before pivoting to banking. This is the invest-vs-execute feeling the run is built on.

**Where the shop lives** is an open fork — free between-gate shop vs. a journey of stops. See [[open/Journey stops vs free shop]].
