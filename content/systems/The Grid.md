# The Grid

> A 4×4 board of goods — the surface you load [[systems/Heat|heat]] onto. Static (no movement); cards target it by position or by type.

## How it works

The board is a 4×4 grid of 16 goods. Each good has a **type** (silk / spice / tea / salt — placeholders) and a **base value** (1–3), plus a **heat** counter starting at 0. Goods are dealt from a goods deck — the *situation* luck hands you; your skill is what you play onto it (see [[meta/Pillars]]). When a heated good sells, its slot refills with a fresh good.

**Static, not spatial.** Goods never move. A fixed grid navigated with a d-pad does **not** violate [[decisions/Controller-first, non-spatial]] — what that decision rejected was *freeform drag-and-drop placement*, not a fixed selectable board.

**Two targeting axes.** Because goods carry both a position and a type, [[systems/Action Deck|cards]] can slice the board different ways: one cell, a row/column/cross, all of a type, or neighbours. The same 16 goods read differently depending on your hand — that's the read-the-board puzzle.
