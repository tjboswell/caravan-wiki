# Selling

> The trigger. Cash every heated good for points, then refill the emptied slots. Gated by a cooldown, so you **load over several rounds, then pop** — the load-now-cash-later rhythm.

## How it works

A **Sell-Off** cashes every good with heat: payout = sum of `base × (1 + heat)` across heated cells, times your sell multiplier from the [[systems/The Shop|shop]]. Sold cells clear and refill from the goods deck; unheated goods stay. Payout becomes [[decisions/Points are score and shop currency|points]] — which are both [[systems/Gates|gate]] score and shop currency (see [[decisions/Points are score and shop currency]]).

**Cooldown.** After a Sell you can't sell again for a couple of rounds. This forces multi-round planning — build a fat board, then release — and creates space for the double-pop combo (cards that reset or reduce the cooldown are their own build axis).

**No penalty for holding** — see [[decisions/No overheat, greed is not punished]]. The reason to cash isn't fear; it's that you need points to afford the upgrades the [[systems/Gates|gates]] demand.
