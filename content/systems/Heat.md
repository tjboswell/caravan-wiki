# Heat

> The stored, inert substance you pile onto goods. It does nothing on its own — value lives in building it up and then cashing it via [[systems/Selling]].

## How it works

Heat is a number on each good. A good's sale value is `base × (1 + heat)`. The three verbs:

- **Stack** — add heat (cheap single appliers, bigger appliers, area appliers). The slow build; individually boring by design.
- **Multiply** — double or scale an existing pile. The break that turns linear into exponential; the combo payoff.
- **Spread** — heat moves between goods (to neighbours, to all of a type, to the whole board). Keeps a contagion feel without needing positions.

**No ceiling, no decay.** Heat is never punished for being large — see [[decisions/No overheat, greed is not punished]]. The tension lives in [[systems/Gates|gate]] demand and the same-pool [[systems/The Shop|economy]], not in a bust.
