# No combat

> Conflict — if any — is economic and logistical, not a health bar. There is no combat in this game.

## Reasoning

Combat would change the game's identity entirely: a health bar introduces a survival layer, stat management, and a whole separate design space. The run is interesting because it's a *trade puzzle*, not an action game or RPG. This constraint also supports the boardable filter (see [[decisions/Boardable as a design filter]]) — a boardgame version of this game has no combat. Conflict and tension come from economic scarcity (limited actions, limited time, competing contracts).

## Rejected

- **Bandit encounters** — a health bar and damage model; wrong genre.
- **Combat mini-game** — a separate mechanical system that dilutes the trade puzzle identity.
- **Health bar** — any health system implies survival, penalty for failure, and combat-adjacent design.

## Affects

- [[systems/The Run]] — all tension comes from economic/logistical constraints, not combat
