# Points are score and shop currency

> One pool. Selling earns **points**; points both count toward the [[systems/Gates|gate]] and are what you spend in the [[systems/The Shop|shop]].

## Reasoning

With the hub gone, "runs supply specific materials to feed an engine" has no engine to feed. Instead, selling pays points, and those same points are the shop currency. Because the pool is shared, every point spent on an upgrade is a point *not* counting toward the gate — a sharp invest-vs-bank tension (the Century-style invest-vs-execute feeling, preserved). Separate money/score rails (Balatro-style) were considered but make shopping pure upside; the same-pool model was chosen deliberately for the tension, accepting the risk that spending must visibly pay back or it feels like losing ground.

## Rejected

- **Separate money and score rails** — shopping never competes with progress; less tension.
- **Specific-materials currency** — the old hub-coupling currency; no hub to couple to.
