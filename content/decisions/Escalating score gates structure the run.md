# Escalating score gates structure the run

> A run is a series of **gates** (Balatro blinds): reach a rising point target within a round budget. Miss it and the run ends; clear it and the target jumps. Score = how deep you got.

## Reasoning

Gates supply the demand that makes the engine matter — the pressure is "is my build big enough," a floor to clear, not a ceiling to fear. Later gates are tuned to be unreachable with a starter deck, so upgrades stop being optional. This is what gives a reason to sell and reinvest early. It also gives a clean fail state without a wipe-in-the-old-sense: you always keep your unlocked content ([[systems/Unlock Pool]]); a run just ends.

## Rejected

- **Fixed-length route with end score** — softer; no escalating demand to force engine growth.
- **Endless no-fail** — no stakes; nothing forces the reinvest decision.
