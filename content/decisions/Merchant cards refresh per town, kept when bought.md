# Merchant cards refresh per town, kept when bought

> Each town offers a fresh set of local merchant cards; anything you buy travels with you for the rest of the run.

## Reasoning

Refreshing per town models the "new locals in a new town" reality and gives every stop a fresh decision surface — there's always something new to evaluate. Keeping bought cards means your owned engine of conversions accumulates across the run, creating a satisfying arc from improvised early-run to humming machine by the end. This is the Century: Spice Road–style accumulation.

## Rejected

- **Persistent shop across all towns** — the same cards always available removes variety and the "new town" feel.
- **Full reset including bought cards** — losing your engine at each town destroys the accumulation arc; your run has no memory.
