# No overheat, greed is not punished

> There is no bust. Loading big is never punished — the giant cash-in is the joy, not a risk. Tension comes from gate **demand** and the same-pool **economy**, not from a ceiling.

## Reasoning

An overheat/scorch bust was prototyped in concept and rejected: it is a *ceiling that punishes going big*, the exact opposite of the Balatro *floor that demands going big*. The million-point turn is the whole reward; punishing it kills the feeling the game is built around. The "why cash before the goal" problem is answered instead by [[decisions/Points are score and shop currency|the same-pool economy]] (you must sell to afford the upgrades you can't win without) and [[decisions/Escalating score gates structure the run|hungry gates]] — all carrot, no burn. A risk-based "manage a volatile substance" variant is a genuinely different game, parked for maybe-someday.

## Rejected

- **Overheat / scorch** — punishes big loads; fights the core joy.
- **Heat decay** — nags; erodes the satisfying build.
- **Forced sell quota** — structural pressure, but redundant given hungry gates.
