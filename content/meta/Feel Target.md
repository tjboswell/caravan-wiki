# Feel Target

> The emotional registers the game must hit.

## The targets

**[[references/Balatro]]'s "it's going exponential" grin**
The [[systems/Heat|multiply]] step, made literal. A board that was a slow pile suddenly doubles and spreads into a detonation. Late in a gate you should feel the acceleration and lean forward.

**A satisfying build-and-release loop**
The moment-to-moment act of loading heat should feel good to *do*, not just to complete: stacking, watching a spread cascade across the grid, lining up the perfect [[systems/Selling|Sell]]. (Replaces the old Century convert-up feel; the pleasure is now build-then-pop.)

**"Watch it scale" — now inside the run**
[[references/Gnorp Apologue|Gnorp's]] scaling pleasure relocated from an idle hub into your own engine: multipliers compounding, pops getting fatter as you reinvest. You feel the snowball turn by turn.

**A merchant's "time the commitment" nerve**
The invest-vs-bank call every round — spend points to grow the engine, or bank them to clear the [[systems/Gates|gate]]? Push for a bigger pop, or cash now? Read-the-situation skill, not combat skill.
