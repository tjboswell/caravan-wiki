# Melvor Idle

> A TypeScript/web RuneScape-inspired idle game that shipped successfully on Steam. The stack precedent.

## What we borrow

- **Stack validation:** Melvor Idle proves that a TS/web game (React-adjacent architecture) can ship on Steam and find a commercial audience in the idle/incremental genre. This is the precedent that de-risks our tech stack choice — see [[decisions/Tech stack: React/Zustand + Pixi]] and [[meta/Tech Stack]].

## What we deliberately DON'T take

- **Mechanical design:** Melvor Idle is a RuneScape clone — skill grinds, massive breadth, leveling. Nothing from its mechanics carries over.
- **Scope:** Melvor Idle is enormous. This game is deliberately scoped small (see [[meta/Prototype Scope]]).
