# Century Spice Road

> A boardgame about trading and converting spice cubes along a route of cards. The run's direct ancestor.

## What we borrow

- **The cargo-conversion engine:** cards that convert one type of good into another, bought and accumulated across a run.
- **The invest-vs.-execute tension:** buying a card and using a card both cost an action (our core turn economy — see [[decisions/Actions limited per town, buy and use both cost]]).
- **Accumulation arc:** your engine starts thin and grows into a humming machine across the run.
- The run's atom: Century is essentially what one run of this game feels like, before all the other layers.

## What we deliberately DON'T take

- **Full upfront market:** Century's card row is always visible and stable. We swap this for per-town refresh with kept purchases — variety + accumulation.
- **Scoring tracks:** Century's endgame is pure optimization to a visible point target. Ours adds the route (push-your-luck) and hub coupling as the destination.
- **Multiplayer/race dynamic:** Century is a competitive race. This game is single-player with no opponent pressure.
