# What an action can do

> What is the complete verb list for a single action?

## Resolved

- **Buy a merchant card** (costs 1 action) — confirmed
- **Use/activate a merchant card** (costs 1 action) — converts or upgrades cargo; confirmed
- **Fulfill a contract** (costs 1 action) — **resolved: yes, fulfilling costs an action** (default yes)

## New verbs in play

- **Buy a relic** (costs 1 action + cargo) — spending cargo mid-area to purchase a persistent modifier from [[systems/Relics]]
- **Leave / travel** (free for now) — moving to the next town is not an action spend in the current prototype
- **Accept a contract** (proposed) — separately committing to a contract before fulfilling it; not yet locked

## Still open

- Can you pass on a remaining action?
- Are all merchant card activations a single action, or do some cost more?
- Are there other verbs — discarding cards, trading directly, scouting?

## Candidate: variable action cost / time-track

A **Patchwork-style time-track** (variable action cost per verb) has been raised as an alternative to a flat per-town action count. Under this model, some verbs are cheap (1 "tick") and others expensive (2–3 ticks), with your total budget being a track rather than a count. This would make the action economy more textured without adding more actions overall. Not locked — prototype with flat costs first.

## Notes

This is a **structural question** — the verb list defines the complete possibility space of a turn. It cannot be resolved by theorizing alone; it must emerge from playtesting. The prototype should start with the minimal verb set (buy, use, fulfill) and expand only when a felt need arises.

The action count per town (~3, see [[open/Actions per town and towns per run]]) cannot be tuned in isolation from the verb list — both define the turn economy together.
