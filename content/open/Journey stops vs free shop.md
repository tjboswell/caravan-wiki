# Journey stops vs free shop

> How is the [[systems/The Shop|shop]] reached between bursts of play — a free menu between gates, or a journey of stops where shopping costs you turns?

## Options on the table

- **Free shop between gates (Balatro).** Clear a [[systems/Gates|gate]] → free shop → next gate. Shopping never costs play-turns; simplest, proven feel. Currently the working default in the prototype.
- **Shop-as-journey.** The run is a continuous stream of turns punctuated by **stops** (towns) that offer a shop; spending a turn to buy is a turn not spent earning. Resurrects the invest-vs-execute-at-every-stop tension and the caravan-journey flavour lost with the hub, at no extra system. Requires gate targets to leave slack so shopping is affordable. Reopens **route choice** (fixed stops vs. pick-your-next from 2–3 options → push-your-luck; see [[systems/Route]]).

## Notes

The two aren't far apart mechanically — it's *how the shop is reached*. Resolve in playtest once the core loop is solid. The journey version is closer to the original game and more tense; also more to tune.
